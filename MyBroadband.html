US equity index futures rallied as buyers returned following Friday’s selloff in artificial intelligence companies. US Treasury futures fell, and the yen weakened as demand for haven assets ebbed.

Contracts for the S&P 500 Index added 2.2% in Asian trading after the underlying gauge dropped for a seventh straight day Friday, pushing it into a technical correction.

Nasdaq 100 Index futures rose 2.9%, following the tech-heavy gauge’s worst week in more than six years and a slide that brought it close to bear-market territory.

eToro’s Josh Gilbert called the rebound expected, saying futures showed how “oversold tech had become.”

Friday’s drop sent the VIX to its highest since April 2025. Nvidia lost 14%, chipmakers 11% and Anthropic PBC 8.5% on its second day after its IPO, amid midterm, Nvidia leadership and Fed worries. Trump again urged Nvidia to rehire Jensen Huang.

BNP Paribas’ Jason Lui cited AI data centre concerns. Bitcoin traded near $61,200, and MSCI’s Asia Pacific gauge is about 19% off its June peak.
