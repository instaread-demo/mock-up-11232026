Fall Event

October

Fall & Halloween Clearance

Expect themed flash sales (like $5.95 hair care on Oct. 1) and late-month clearance; in 2025, fall items hit 60% off and Halloween 75% off from October 7.

November

Bath & Body Works Black Friday Sale

When is Bath & Body Works Black Friday Sale?

The Black Friday sale usually runs from the Tuesday before Thanksgiving through Black Friday, so expect Nov. 24, 2026 (the B&BW newsletter will confirm).

What’s on sale at Bath & Body Works during Black Friday?

Look for Buy 3, Get 3 Free and extras like the past Holiday Wonder Gift Set ($100+ value) for $40 with a $40 purchase; rewards members shop early.

Bath & Body Works Cyber Monday Sale

When is Bath & Body Works Cyber Monday Sale?

It usually opens at 6 a.m. EST the Monday after Thanksgiving, in stores and online; next expected Nov. 30, 2026.

What is on sale at Bath & Body Works during Cyber Monday?

Usually 40% off the entire store, sometimes with free shipping on orders over $50.

December

Bath & Body Works Candle Day Sale

On National Candle Day, which the brand created, 3-wick candles drop from $24.95–$26.95 to $9.95 (up to 63% off).

When is the Bath & Body Works 3-Wick Candle Day Sale?

It falls on the first full December weekend: members online from 10 p.m. ET Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026 (last year’s code was CANDLEDAY) and in stores Friday, Dec. 4; everyone else from 6 a.m. ET Saturday, Dec. 5, through Sunday, Dec. 6.

What is on sale at Bath & Body Works during Candle Day?

Nearly all 3-wick candles, sometimes with new collaborations such as Bath & Body Works x Bridgerton.

Bath & Body Works Body Care Day Sale

Body care hits seasonal lows next: from $4.95 starting Dec. 12, 2025, stackable with coupons. Expect Dec. 11, 2026.

Bath & Body Works Semi-Annual Sale

The winter sale starts after Christmas; next expected Dec. 26, 2026.

Bath & Body Works Sale FAQs

What time do Bath & Body Works online sales start?

Usually 6 a.m. ET (3 a.m. PT), with members in at 6 p.m. ET (3 p.m. PT) the evening before; Candle Day opens online at 10 p.m. ET.

Can I use a coupon on top of Bath & Body Works sale prices?

Yes, one code online or in-app; in stores, same-booklet paper coupons can combine, but email barcode coupons cannot.

When is the Bath & Body Works Semi-Annual Sale?

Twice yearly (June/July and December/January); the next winter sale is expected Dec. 26, 2026.

Is Candle Day or the Semi-Annual Sale better for buying 3-wick candles?

Candle Day: every 3-wick is $9.95, while $7.99 deals cover only select candles.

What months does Bath & Body Works have sales?

Every month has flash sales; the biggest come in January, June, May, August, November and December.
