Trump says meeting with NK leader will happen 'at some point': report

US President Donald Trump said Thursday he will meet with North Korean leader Kim Jong-un "at some point" in an interview with NBC after a highly speculated meeting between the two did not happen during Trump's Asia trip this week.

"The relationship is there, but we didn't meet," Trump was quoted as saying by an NBC reporter on X. "There were too many meetings. ... It wouldn't have worked out. ... We'll have that meeting at some point."

Trump returned from APEC in Shenzhen, China; the trip, where he had floated meeting Kim, also included Manila's ASEAN summit.

Marco Rubio called him "serious" about engaging Kim. (Yonhap)
