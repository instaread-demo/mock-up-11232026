Kansas City Current Win First Championship Over Washington Spirit

The Current defeated the Spirit 2-1 in Washington D.C.

For the first time, the Kansas City Current are the NWSL Champions.

In a game fought to the very end, the Current defeated the Washington Spirit, 2-1, at Audi Field in the 2026 NWSL Championship presented by Google Pixel.

After returning from maternity leave this summer, it was Vanessa DiBernardo who was the hero and the match MVP. DiBernardo broke through with the game’s first goal in the 54th minute – an Olimpico that curled into the back post.

Current captain Loʻeau LaBonta dubbed her “MomVP.”

LaBonta called her a fellow leader and the goal “saucy.”

Debinha doubled the lead in second-half stoppage time as the Spirit pushed forward.

It proved the winner; Debinha now owns four playoff game-winners, three of them in championship matches.

Trinity Rodman scored for Washington nine minutes into stoppage time, too late to change the result.

Midfielder of the Year Croix Bethune, a Spirit player for two seasons, won her first title against her old club.

Haley Hopkins credited the traveling fans’ “wave of teal.”
