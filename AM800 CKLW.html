35-year-old man killed in east Windsor crash

By Dustin Coffman

Updated: November 22, 2026 at 6:18a.m. EST

Published: November 21, 2026 at 10:20a.m. EST

A 35-year-old man is dead following a single-vehicle collision in Windsor late Friday night.

Windsor police say the crash happened shortly before midnight near Wyandotte Street East and Drouillard Road. Officers arrived to find a severely damaged sedan that had struck the concrete wall of an underpass.

According to police, the vehicle was travelling westbound on Wyandotte at a high rate of speed, went through a red light and narrowly avoided another vehicle before crashing into the wall.

The driver died in hospital. While the Traffic Reconstruction Unit investigated, Drouillard (Edna Street to Riverside Drive East) and Wyandotte (Albert Road to Cadillac Street) were closed; both have reopened.

Witnesses can call the Traffic Enforcement Unit at 519-255-6700, ext. 7032.
