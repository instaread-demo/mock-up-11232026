Amy Sherman-Palladino’s 2006 exit from Gilmore Girls left a gaping hole in Stars Hollow.

In Drink Coffee, Talk Fast: A Documentary About Gilmore Girls, cast and crew say her departure over a network contract changed the set as well as the reviews.

Director Jamie Babbit says producer David Rosenthal let the actors lead, and they wanted to wrap on time; costumer Valerie Campbell notes Sherman-Palladino had fought the network for resources.

By then the show, with Lauren Graham and Alexis Bledel as Lorelai and Rory, had passed 100 episodes; Babbit thinks Bledel was relieved after six years.

Michael Winters (Taylor Doose) recalls a quicker, “close enough” pace; Kelly Bishop (Emily Gilmore) calls it a weaker season without its creator, but Babbit recalls it differently.
