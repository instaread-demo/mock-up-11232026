‘Every inch’: Rutte delivers chilling warning to Putin as Russian incursions raise fears of NATO war

NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte has pledged that the alliance will defend “every inch” of Polish territory as suspected Russian incursions intensify fears of a direct confrontation between Moscow and the military bloc.

The warning comes amid mounting tensions along NATO’s eastern flank, with Poland reporting suspected Russian missile and drone activity over its territory and accusing Moscow of attempting to destabilise the alliance through covert operations.

Rutte spoke before NATO convenes in Brussels on Monday. Warsaw also says it foiled an alleged Russian false-flag plot in Kaliningrad.

Warsaw reported two Russian missiles and a drone crossing into Poland on Friday, one a cruise missile that hit the rural south; Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz says the alliance will step up support.
