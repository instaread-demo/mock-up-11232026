UAE petrol prices for December 2026: Will fuel costs drop again?

Fuel prices could fall in December as global oil prices ease, although the UAE Fuel Price Committee has yet to announce the official rates

Motorists in the UAE could see lower petrol and diesel prices in December 2026, with declining international oil prices raising expectations of a second consecutive monthly reduction at the pumps.

The UAE Fuel Price Committee is expected to announce the new rates on Monday, November 30, with the revised prices taking effect from Tuesday, December 1.

Official rates are pending, but Brent crude slipped under $100 a barrel in November from about $110 in October, per market reports; the committee’s monthly review will settle the final change.

Will UAE petrol prices fall in December?

Markets.com’s November 18 analysis forecast Special 95 at Dhs3.55 to Dhs3.83 (now Dhs4.04), Super 98 at Dhs3.65 to Dhs3.95, E-Plus 91 at Dhs3.45 to Dhs3.76 and diesel at Dhs4.15 to Dhs4.60 a litre. These are not official figures.

UAE petrol and diesel prices in November 2026

The UAE cut pump prices in November; the Emirates News Agency (WAM) listed the approved rates:
– Super 98: Dhs4.16 per litre, down from Dhs4.40 in October.
– Special 95: Dhs4.04 per litre, down from Dhs4.28.
– E-Plus 91: Dhs3.97 per litre, down from Dhs4.21.
– Diesel: Dhs4.75 per litre, down from Dhs4.80.

That was 5.6 per cent lower for petrol and 1.04 per cent for diesel, per WAM.

When will UAE December fuel prices be announced?

Prices have been set monthly since 2015. December rates are due November 30, effective December 1; until then, a cut is only a forecast.
